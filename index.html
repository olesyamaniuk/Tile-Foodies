How to Play

Pro Tip: Save your boosters for challenging levels, and complete daily quests to earn extra rewards!

  • Match & Collect:

    Tap on three identical food tiles to match and clear the board.

  • Unlock Boosters:

    Use power-ups like mixers, ovens, and chef specials to clear levels faster.

  • Build & Customize:

    Earn rewards to decorate homes, unlock new foodie areas, and personalize the town.

  • Upgrade Your Foodies:

    Collect foodie cards to level up their skills and unlock abilities!

  • Discover New Locations: 

    Explore beautiful villages, forests, and markets as you progress.

FAQ

  • What is Tile Foodies: Match & Collect?
    It's a match-3 puzzle game where you match food tiles, collect unique foodie characters, and build an adorable town.
  • How do I unlock new Foodies?
    Complete levels, participate in events, and collect foodie cards to unlock new characters.
  • Can I decorate my Foodies' homes?
    Yes! Earn decorative items, upgrade houses, and create beautiful custom spaces for your foodie friends.
  • Do I need an internet connection to play?
    No, you can enjoy offline gameplay, but some events and competitions require an internet connection.
  • Are there any power-ups?
    Absolutely! Use bombs, shuffles, and boosters to tackle difficult levels.
  • Can I compete with my friends?
    Yes! Connect your game to Facebook and see who's the best foodie collector!
  • What happens if I run out of moves?
    You can use special boosters, watch ads for extra moves, or restart the level and try a new strategy.
  • Are there regular updates?
    Yes! Expect new levels, seasonal events, and fresh decorations in every update.
